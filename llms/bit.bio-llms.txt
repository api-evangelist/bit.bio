# bit.bio

> bit.bio is a Cambridge, UK synthetic biology company that deterministically programs
> human induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) into defined human cell types using its
> proprietary opti-ox cell coding technology, and sells them as ioCells for research,
> drug discovery and cell therapy. bit.bio publishes no public API, SDK, developer
> portal or machine-readable API contract — its products are physical cryopreserved
> cell vials sold through a web storefront and a distributor network.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from publicly reachable bit.bio pages. It is
not served by bit.bio; https://www.bit.bio/llms.txt returned 404 on 2026-08-07.

## Company

- [bit.bio](https://www.bit.bio/): Synthetic biology for human health. Founded 2016 by Mark Kotter; HQ at the Babraham Research Campus, Cambridge, UK, with a site in San Francisco.
- [About us](https://www.bit.bio/about-us): Company background and leadership.
- [Platform](https://www.bit.bio/platform): The opti-ox cell coding platform — dual genomic safe harbour targeting for deterministic forward programming of iPSCs.
- [bit.bio discovery](https://bitbiodiscovery.com/): Vienna-based subsidiary (bit.bio discovery GmbH) combining single-cell functional genomics with cellular models for target discovery.
- [Therapeutics pipeline](https://www.bit.bio/therapeutics-pipeline): Cell therapy programmes.

## Products

- [Products](https://www.bit.bio/products): Full ioCells catalogue — neurons, microglia, hepatocytes, glial and muscle lineages.
- [Discover ioCells](https://www.bit.bio/discover-iocells): ioWild Type Cells, ioDisease Model Cells and CRISPR-Ready ioCells.
- [Custom cell development](https://www.bit.bio/products/custom-cell-development): Bespoke iPSC-derived cell lines.
- [CRISPR screens](https://www.bit.bio/crispr-screens): Expert-led CRISPR functional genomics screening service.
- [Shop](https://shop.bit.bio/): E-commerce storefront (Shopify-hosted) for ordering cell vials.
